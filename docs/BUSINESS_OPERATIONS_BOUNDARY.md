# Business Operations public boundary

## Purpose

CLARYEL Web Community remains a standalone public voice-first website-creation product. It does not become a public mirror of private Business Operations data or require `claryel-operations` at build time.

## Future public-safe integration

After the managed private implementation is tested, legally reviewed and sanitized, Community may expose optional generic contracts or templates that let an independently deployed website connect to a user-selected business backend.

Public-safe candidates may include:

- generic lead/contact submission contract;
- generic product/service catalogue query contract;
- generic quotation/order-status presentation schema;
- generic support-entry contract;
- generic consent and data-minimization guidance;
- synthetic Business Instance integration examples containing no private CLARYEL topology or customer data.

## Prohibited transfer

The public repository must not receive:

- real Business Instance or Legal Entity records;
- CLARYEL customer, employee, order, payment, tax or banking data;
- private CRM/ERP endpoints or topology;
- ServiceHub physical inventory details beyond explicitly published synthetic/public-safe schemas;
- provider credentials or private integration configuration;
- internal pricing, financing decisions or commercial support records.

## Product relationship

The packaged operating-company product can use the managed private website platform to generate a customer website and portal. Web Community remains the independently buildable public website-creation edition. Shared generic contracts cross the boundary only after explicit public-safety review.

This preserves the long-term possibility that a Community-created website can connect to a CLARYEL Business Operations deployment without making the public software dependent on the private platform.
